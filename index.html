The Story

Before the Journey

Beyond the familiar horizon, the world had become a place of startling contrasts. Technology promised comfort, yet the natural world had grown increasingly distant from ordinary life.

For Michelle and Matthew, a journey would raise questions neither had expected to face.

Their story begins with a world that believes it has mastered nature—and with the possibility that nature still has secrets to reveal.

The Story

A Changing World

There were places people spoke about but rarely saw. Behind the order of daily routines lay the memory of forests, rivers, and a different way of living.

Some regarded those memories as relics. Others wondered whether they were a warning.

What Michelle and Matthew would discover could change how they understood the world around them.
